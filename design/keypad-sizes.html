Quantus handheld: key sizes

Both variants at one scale, drawn from the numbers in the DEVICES table at the bottom of this file. Change a number, reload. Labels are placeholders, the sizes are the point.

SHIFTALPHAUNITEXEbig unit only
  • EXE replaces “=” in the bottom right of the number block. The equals sign still exists as a key for equations (Key::Equals), here in the function block.
  • Soft keys: the top key row is labelled in the upper strip of the screen, the second row in the strip below it (blue tint on both). The strips can be hidden in modes that don't use them (LGS, graph).
  • Dashed keys exist only on the big unit; each one is a shortcut for something the small unit reaches through a menu or the soft keys.
